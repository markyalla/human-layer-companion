THE FIVE PEOPLE (about 1:10). Read one line each, let the room read the rest. Ama, the Master: "Her sign-off is trusted. She is not the one to rush." Kofi, the Storyteller: "He wins the room. Someone needs to hold the details." Yaw, the Metronome: "He is the one you trust with the long job." Efua, the Sprinter: "She is the one you call when it has to move now." Selorm, the Pathfinder: "He is the one you send when nobody knows the way yet." Say: "Top half of each card is measured, the same for everyone. The bottom half is character: how they like to work. A low bar is not a flaw." Then: "Who would you hire?" Pause. "Trick question. It depends on the job, and what the job needs around it. Most of us hire the same one every time." All five are invented. Speed, accuracy and free time feed the match in the demo. On time is shown for context and is not used in the match yet. Each has strengths and trade-offs on purpose: nobody is better or worse, only better or worse for a particular job. Never say one is the obvious choice. The real vote is later (Live 2); this question is to make the room think. One sentence on "game": chess, not Flappy Bird.
