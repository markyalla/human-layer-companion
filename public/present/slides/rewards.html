REWARDS (about 0:30). "This is where gamification earns its place. As someone moves through onboarding and the work, they get fair rewards for progress: for being consistent, for speaking up early. It makes them feel seen, because the system shows their progress to them and to their manager. And it is customised: what counts as progress in your business is yours to define." Applies to customer onboarding too: welcome, engagement, retention. Say "we aim to"; do not claim retention results. The five steps are an illustration of a reward path, not a built feature.
