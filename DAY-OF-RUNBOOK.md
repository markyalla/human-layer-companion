# Day-of runbook (print this)

## Three screens, three links
- **Audience phones:** scan the QR on the opening join slide (or the printed backup). Name, two taps, keep the page open.
- **Your tablet (private):** https://YOUR-ADDRESS/present/CODE . Sign in with your password. Notes: the Notes button (or N). Do not mirror this screen.
- **Projector laptop (public view):** the "Projector link" button on the tablet copies it: https://YOUR-ADDRESS/show/CODE?k=KEY . No password, no notes, no buttons. Double-click once for full screen and sound.

## Set up (30 minutes before)
1. https://YOUR-ADDRESS/host , sign in, make the session. Privacy "named" if you want names for the golden apples; "aggregate" hides names (winners show as Participant plus a code). Put the room size in "Taking part".
2. Tablet: open /present/CODE, sign in, tap Projector link.
3. Projector laptop: open that link, double-click, check the reel plays with sound (press the right arrow on the tablet).
4. Phone test: scan the QR with one phone, join, confirm the join count goes up on the projector.
5. Close the test: end the session and make a fresh one if you tested on the real code.

## Keys on the tablet
Right/Left arrow: next/back. R: reveal (apples, results). O: open the current question. C: close it. I: show or hide the interest count on the offer slide. The projector follows. N: notes. F: full screen.

## After (5 minutes, in this order)
1. Leave the session open about two minutes for late taps.
2. ADDRESS/host, your session, Export: download Leads, Responses, Participants, Summary and Everything (JSON).
3. Open Leads. Rows = everyone who chose an interest option except "Not right now". Rows should equal the submitted count on the host page minus the "Not right now" count.
4. Copy all the files to two places. Only then think about deleting the session.
5. Follow up within 48 hours. Details: FINAL-DEPLOY-AND-DATA.md, sections C and C3.
